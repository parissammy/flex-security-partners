How you get paid

The $5,000 example

Let's say you sell a customer a $5,000 security camera system. First, we take 35% of the sale. That's $1,750. Next, we subtract the cost of the cameras. Four cameras cost $500 total. Then we subtract $400 to pay the technicians to install them, and $285 for the NVR recorder.

$1,750 minus $500 minus $400 minus $285 = $565.

$565 to your call center

The more deals you close, the more money you make.

The formula on every deal: 35% of the sale, minus $125 per camera, minus $100 per installed camera, minus $285 for the NVR. Every deal has an NVR.

Bigger system example (ESTIMATED): an $8,000 system with 6 cameras. 35% of $8,000 is $2,800. Six cameras cost $750. Install for six is $600. The NVR is $285. $2,800 minus $750 minus $600 minus $285 = $1,165 to your call center.

Commission only. Paid after the customer's deal funds. No upfront pay either way: you never pay us, and we don't pay a base or setup fee.
